Eligibility decision-support

Clinical Trial Matcher

Paste a short patient summary. An AI agent retrieves candidate trials from public ClinicalTrials.gov data and judges each eligibility criterion as met, not met, or unknown, citing the evidence and abstaining when the summary doesn't say.

Who it's for: built as decision-support for clinicians and trial coordinators screening patients. It deliberately flags what it can't verify, so a human makes the call.

Prototype, not a clinical tool. Built on public trial data for demonstration. Do not enter real patient identifiers. Not for clinical decisions.

How it works. Each request runs a self-correcting agent:
1 · Analyze2 · Retrieve trials 3 · Grade relevance4 · Judge criteria 5 · Verify evidence

A deterministic verification step downgrades any verdict that isn't grounded in the patient summary. So, the model can't claim what it can't cite.
